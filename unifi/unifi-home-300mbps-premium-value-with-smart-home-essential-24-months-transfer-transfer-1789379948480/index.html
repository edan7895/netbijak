The Important Part: This Is a Transfer, Not a New Broadband Application

This product is designed for customers who already have broadband with another provider and want to switch to Unifi.

Unifi's official process is:

  1. Request the Transfer at a Unifi Store, TMpoint or authorised reseller.
  2. Select the preferred Unifi Home Broadband plan.
  3. Unifi raises the transfer request and provides a Transfer Request ID.
  4. Settle outstanding amounts with the existing provider, including any applicable early termination or contract penalty charges.
  5. Wait for approval from the existing provider.
  6. Unifi then contacts you to arrange the installation.

Unifi states that approval from the existing provider may take up to 10 days.

Do Not Cancel Your Existing Broadband Too Early

This is the most important practical point when switching.

Do not terminate your existing broadband before the Unifi Home Fibre installation is completed.

Unifi specifically advises customers not to terminate the existing service until the Unifi installation has been completed, to avoid an unnecessary loss of internet connectivity during the transition.

In other words, the safer sequence is:

Transfer request → existing provider approval → Unifi installation → confirm Unifi service works → terminate/complete the old service transition

rather than cancelling your existing broadband first and waiting for the new installation.

Check Your Existing Contract Before Switching

Unifi's Transfer FAQ specifically tells customers to settle outstanding bills with their current provider, including any early termination or contract penalty charges, before the transfer request is submitted.

This means the real switching cost is not necessarily just the Unifi bill.

For a customer still locked into an existing broadband contract, the calculation should be:

Existing ISP termination cost + Unifi 24-month commitment

That is the number worth checking before accepting the offer.

FAQ

Is the Unifi Home 300Mbps Transfer plan for existing Unifi customers?

No. In this context, Transfer refers to customers switching their existing broadband service from another provider to Unifi. Unifi's official FAQ has a dedicated process for transferring an existing broadband service from another provider.

Is RM59/month available for all 24 months?

No. The offer provides RM59/month for the first 10 months. The remaining 14 months must be calculated using the applicable rate for the offer.

How much do I pay during the first 10 months?

RM590 in total, calculated as:

RM59 × 10 = RM590

What happens after the first 10 months?

The monthly charge changes to the applicable rate stated under the offer. Therefore, the full 24-month cost cannot be calculated from the RM59 promotional price alone.

Can I cancel my old broadband immediately after applying for the Unifi Transfer?

You should not. Unifi specifically advises customers to keep their existing broadband active until the Unifi Home Fibre installation has been completed to avoid service disruption.

How long can the transfer approval take?

Unifi states that approval from the existing provider may take up to 10 days before Unifi proceeds with the installation appointment.

Do I have to pay my existing ISP's early termination charges?

If your current broadband contract is still active, you may have early termination or other outstanding charges. Unifi's Transfer FAQ instructs customers to settle outstanding bills, including applicable early termination or contract penalty charges, before submitting the transfer request.

What Smart Home device is included?

This offer includes 1 AI Indoor Camera. Unifi's Smart Home information describes the camera as supporting 2K QHD video, 360° rotation and AI detection features.
