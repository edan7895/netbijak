✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent fit for medium-sized households where multiple people frequently stream high-definition content, game, or work from home simultaneously. By bundling a Netflix subscription directly into your bill, it simplifies your entertainment costs, though the two-year lock-in period means you should be certain about your long-term housing stability before signing up. While 500Mbps is more than enough for most families, keep in mind that the upload speed is significantly lower than the download; if you are a heavy content creator who frequently uploads large video files to the cloud, you may eventually find that limit restrictive. Before committing, verify your area’s infrastructure quality, as the provided Wi-Fi 6 hardware performs best in homes where the router is centrally located away from thick physical obstructions.
