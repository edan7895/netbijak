RM119.83/month effective broadband + Smart Home package cost

This Is a Transfer Product — Check Your Existing ISP First

The main issue with a Transfer package is not the application itself. It is what happens to your existing broadband contract.

If your current ISP contract has not ended, check whether switching will trigger:

  1. Early termination charges
  2. Outstanding bills
  3. Remaining contractual obligations

You should calculate these costs separately from the Unifi package.

Your real switching cost is therefore:

Existing ISP termination cost + RM2,876 Unifi contract cost

A low introductory price does not eliminate the cost of leaving your existing provider early.

Do Not Cancel Your Existing Broadband Before Installation

This is particularly important for a Transfer application.

Do not cancel your existing broadband simply because the Unifi Transfer request has been submitted.

The safer approach is:

Transfer application → approval → Unifi installation → confirm the new connection works → complete the old broadband cancellation

That avoids creating an unnecessary period without internet access.

FAQ

Is RM79/month the price for the entire 24 months?

No.

The promotion is:

RM79/month for the first 10 months, followed by RM149/month for the remaining 14 months.

How much will I pay over 24 months?

The total is:

RM79 × 10 + RM149 × 14

= RM790 + RM2,086

= RM2,876

The effective monthly cost is therefore:

RM2,876 ÷ 24 = RM119.83/month

What is included with the Smart Home Essential package?

The current Unifi Home Shield documentation lists the Essential package with 1 AI Indoor Camera, the Unifi Smart Home app and 7-day video cloud storage.

Can I treat the AI Indoor Camera as RM12 of monthly savings?

Not directly.

Unifi currently lists the AI Indoor Camera as an RM12/month add-on, but the RM12 figure is the add-on price, not necessarily the cash value that should be deducted from the bundled broadband cost.

The cleaner calculation is to use the actual package bill of RM79 / RM149 and treat the camera as an included benefit.

What happens after the 24-month contract?

The promotional contract price should not automatically be assumed to continue after the 24-month period. Unifi's current Home Shield campaign documentation states that its listed discounted bundle prices apply during the 24-month contract period, with retail pricing applying thereafter for that campaign.

For this specific Transfer promotion, check the offer terms shown at the point of subscription for the applicable post-contract price.

What happens if I terminate during the 24-month contract?

Unifi's Home Shield campaign terms state that terminating the bundle during the contract period can trigger an early termination penalty based on the remaining months and applicable bundle price.

That makes the 24-month commitment an important part of the cost calculation, not just a footnote.

Is the AI Indoor Camera permanently mine?

The device may remain with the customer after termination, but Unifi's terms state that the 7-day cloud storage feature for the AI Indoor Camera will be discontinued when the Home Shield package is terminated.
