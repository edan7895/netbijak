✨ NetBijak's Take

This tier is designed for power-user households or tech-heavy homes where multiple people are simultaneously streaming 4K content, gaming, and managing large cloud backups. While the symmetric-adjacent speeds are impressive, keep in mind that you will be locked into a two-year commitment, making it a significant long-term investment. Before committing, ensure your home infrastructure, such as your internal network cables and device hardware, can actually handle these speeds, otherwise, you might be paying for bandwidth your current setup cannot fully utilize.
