✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is a cost-effective choice for small households or light users who primarily handle streaming, web browsing, and remote work without needing heavy bandwidth. While the three-month waiver and included Wi-Fi 6 hardware provide excellent immediate value, keep in mind that you are committing to a 27-month contract, which is slightly longer than the standard two-year term. If your household anticipates needing higher speeds for gaming or 4K streaming in the near future, consider if this entry-level tier will remain sufficient, as upgrading mid-contract can sometimes be less flexible than choosing a higher tier from the start. Before signing up, ensure your residence has a clear path for the installer, as taking advantage of the free setup offer is the best way to maximize the initial savings.
