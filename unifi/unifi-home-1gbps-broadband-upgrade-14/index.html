✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent choice for large households or power users who frequently juggle 4K streaming, heavy cloud backups, and competitive gaming simultaneously. While the inclusion of Wi-Fi 6 mesh hardware significantly boosts coverage in multi-room homes, keep in mind that you are locked into a two-year commitment, making it a serious long-term investment. If your household has fewer than five active users, you might find more cost-effective value in the lower-tier plans, as you likely will not saturate this level of bandwidth. Before signing, ensure your devices actually support Wi-Fi 6 standards, otherwise, you may not experience the full performance benefits promised by the new router.
