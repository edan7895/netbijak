Netflix Basic: What Are You Actually Getting?

Netflix Basic is not the highest Netflix tier.

According to Netflix Malaysia, Basic currently provides:

  1. 720p resolution
  2. 1 device watching at a time
  3. Compatible with phones, tablets, laptops and TVs
  4. Monthly price of RM29.90 when purchased separately

The one-device limit is particularly important.

If one person is watching Netflix on the TV while another household member tries to watch Netflix on a laptop, the Basic plan does not provide two simultaneous streams.

So the question is not simply whether you use Netflix. It is how many people use Netflix at the same time.

The 4K TV Trap

A 4K television does not automatically mean you are getting 4K Netflix.

The bundled plan is Netflix Basic, which is limited to 720p.

If your main reason for choosing the bundle is watching Netflix on a 4K TV, Basic may not match what you expect.

To move from Basic to Premium:

Additional cost = RM33/month

Over a 24-month period:

RM33 × 24 = RM792

So a customer who keeps Premium for the entire 24-month broadband contract would pay an additional RM792 for the Netflix upgrade.

That is worth including in the budget before signing a long-term broadband contract.

What Happens If You Need Two Screens?

The same issue applies to households with multiple Netflix viewers.

Basic allows one device at a time, while Standard allows two devices at a time.

If two people regularly watch Netflix simultaneously, the Basic bundle may force an upgrade.

RM20/month

RM20 × 24 = RM480

For Premium:

RM118.90 + RM33 = RM151.90/month

This is the number worth comparing if your household actually needs 4K/HDR and four simultaneous devices.

Who Should Get the Netflix Basic Bundle?

Consider it if you:

  1. Already intend to subscribe to Netflix Basic.
  2. Usually have only one Netflix viewer at a time.
  3. Are comfortable with 720p.
  4. Do not specifically need 4K Netflix.
  5. Prefer having the broadband and Netflix subscription combined.

Consider upgrading Netflix if you:

  1. Have a 4K TV and want 4K/HDR.
  2. Regularly have two people watching Netflix simultaneously.
  3. Want better picture quality than 720p.
  4. Need more simultaneous devices.

Consider buying Netflix separately if:

  1. You want maximum flexibility over your Netflix subscription.
  2. You do not see any financial advantage in the current bundle price.
  3. You expect to change Netflix tiers frequently.

Contract & Commitment

The broadband package carries a 24-month contract. Unifi's FAQ states that termination after the minimum contract period does not incur a penalty, while early termination is subject to the applicable terms.

This is important when evaluating the Netflix portion of the deal.

Netflix itself is flexible, but the broadband commitment is not equivalent to a month-to-month Netflix subscription.

If you are signing the broadband contract mainly because Netflix is included, make sure the broadband service itself is something you would keep for the full contract period.
