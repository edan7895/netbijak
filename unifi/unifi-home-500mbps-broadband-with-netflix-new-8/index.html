Quick Verdict

The Unifi Home 500Mbps Broadband with Netflix package is a different proposition from Unifi's broadband-only plans: you are paying RM203.90/month for a 500Mbps broadband connection bundled with Netflix Basic on a 24-month contract.

The important question is not simply whether RM203.90 is cheap. It is whether bundling Netflix through Unifi gives you better value than paying for broadband and Netflix separately.

Over 24 months, the package costs:

RM203.90 × 24 = RM4,893.60

The included Netflix Basic plan is the key value component. If you already pay for Netflix, the bundle can make sense. If you do not use Netflix, there is little reason to pay extra for the bundle.

Netflix Basic: Know What You Are Actually Getting

The Netflix component is Basic, so this is not the tier for households that specifically want 4K Netflix.

Netflix Basic is designed around:

  1. 720p resolution
  2. 1 supported screen at a time

That means the bundle's entertainment value depends heavily on whether those limitations match your Netflix usage.

If you only watch Netflix occasionally on one TV or device, Basic may be enough.

If multiple people regularly watch Netflix at the same time, or you specifically want 4K/HDR, you should look at the upgrade cost rather than assuming the included Basic plan is sufficient.

Is Upgrading Netflix Still Worth It?

The important point is that upgrading Netflix changes the monthly bill, but it does not necessarily change the underlying broadband value.

If the Netflix upgrade is charged separately through the Unifi account, calculate it as:

RM203.90 + Netflix upgrade fee

For example, if your chosen upgrade costs an additional RM20/month:

RM203.90 + RM20 = RM223.90/month

Over 24 months:

RM223.90 × 24 = RM5,373.60

So the decision should be based on the Netflix tier you actually need rather than paying for a higher tier simply because the bundle includes Netflix.

Who Should Get the 500Mbps Netflix Bundle?

Consider it if you:

  1. Are applying for a new Unifi 500Mbps connection.
  2. Already pay for Netflix and prefer having the subscription included in the broadband bill.
  3. Are comfortable with a 24-month contract.
  4. Are satisfied with Netflix Basic's 720p / 1-screen limitation.
  5. Prefer a single monthly bill for broadband and Netflix.

Think twice if you:

  1. Do not use Netflix.
  2. Want Netflix 4K/HDR.
  3. Need Netflix on multiple screens simultaneously.
  4. Can purchase the standalone broadband and Netflix Basic for less.
  5. Prefer keeping your streaming subscription independent from your broadband provider.
