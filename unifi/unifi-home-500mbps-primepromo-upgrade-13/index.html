✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan strikes a sweet spot for large households or heavy remote-working families who need reliable bandwidth without paying premium prices. While the three-month waiver makes the initial entry very attractive, be mindful that you are committing to a 27-month contract, so ensure your living situation is stable before signing up. The inclusion of the Wi-Fi 6 router is a significant practical upgrade that helps manage traffic for multiple simultaneous users, but keep in mind that the upload speed is capped at 100Mbps, which may be a slight bottleneck if you frequently upload large video files or host local servers.
