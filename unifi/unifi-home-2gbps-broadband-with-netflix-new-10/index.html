✨ NetBijak's Take

This high-tier plan is an excellent fit for large households with heavy concurrent users, such as those juggling 4K streaming, online gaming, and cloud-heavy professional workloads simultaneously. While the inclusion of a Netflix subscription and upgraded Wi-Fi 6 mesh hardware adds significant value, the 24-month contract is a serious commitment that requires ensuring your home’s existing infrastructure, like your ethernet cables and devices, can actually handle such high speeds to avoid bottlenecks. If your household has fewer than eight active users, you might find that a lower-tier plan provides a more cost-effective experience without sacrificing performance for daily browsing needs. Always verify your specific property’s wiring before signing up, as achieving these top-tier speeds often requires a hardwired connection rather than relying solely on Wi-Fi.
