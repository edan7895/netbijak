That makes this package less about getting the cheapest possible Netflix subscription and more about combining a higher-tier Netflix plan with Unifi's 1Gbps service.

The Wi-Fi 6 Mesh Is a More Important Part of This Package

The biggest difference between this product and a simple 1Gbps broadband subscription is the networking equipment.

A 1Gbps fibre connection does not automatically mean every room in the house will receive close to 1Gbps over Wi-Fi.

Your actual wireless performance depends on the router, mesh placement, device capability, distance, walls and wireless interference.

That is why the included Wi-Fi 6 certified Mesh matters.

For a larger home or a home where the main router cannot provide consistent coverage to all rooms, a mesh system can be more useful than simply having a higher broadband speed.

The key point is that the mesh should be evaluated as part of the package value rather than assuming the 1Gbps line alone explains the RM293.90 monthly price.

1Gbps Does Not Mean Every Device Gets 1Gbps

The package provides a connection of up to 1Gbps, but your individual device may not achieve that speed.

The actual result can be limited by:

  1. Wi-Fi generation
  2. Device network hardware
  3. Ethernet port capability
  4. Router or mesh connection
  5. Wi-Fi signal strength
  6. Distance from the access point
  7. Network congestion

Unifi specifies the service as up to 1Gbps download and up to 500Mbps upload.

For customers buying this package mainly because they want faster Wi-Fi throughout the house, the included mesh equipment may therefore be more important than the 1Gbps headline itself.

Is Netflix the Reason to Pay More?

Not necessarily.

If you already subscribe to Netflix Standard, having Netflix included in the Unifi bill is convenient.

But if you are choosing between broadband packages, do not evaluate the plan purely by asking:

“How much is Netflix worth?”

The more useful question is:

“What am I paying for the 1Gbps connection and Wi-Fi equipment after accounting for Netflix?”

That separates the entertainment component from the network infrastructure component.

This is particularly important because the package costs RM293.90 every month for 24 months, or RM7,053.60 in total.

Who Should Get the 1Gbps Netflix Bundle?

Consider it if you:

  1. Specifically want a 1Gbps fibre connection.
  2. Need higher upload capacity, with Unifi listing up to 500Mbps upload.
  3. Already use or want Netflix Standard.
  4. Have a larger home where additional Wi-Fi coverage is useful.
  5. Want the Wi-Fi equipment bundled with the broadband subscription.
  6. Are comfortable with a 24-month contract.

Think twice if you:

  1. Mainly want Netflix and do not need 1Gbps broadband.
  2. Already have a capable Wi-Fi 6/mesh system.
  3. Live in a smaller home where additional mesh coverage provides little benefit.
  4. Are unlikely to make meaningful use of a 1Gbps connection.
  5. Prefer to keep Netflix separate from your broadband provider.
