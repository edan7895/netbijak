✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent choice for individuals or small households who want the flexibility of a contract-free subscription without sacrificing high-speed connectivity. While the uncapped 5G performance makes it ideal for heavy streaming or remote work, users should keep in mind that performance will drop to the 100GB 4G threshold once they move out of 5G coverage areas. Given its plug-and-play nature, it is a perfect temporary solution for renters or those who do not want to commit to long-term fiber installations. Before signing up, ensure your primary device is 5G compatible and verify the 5G signal strength in your specific room to get the most consistent experience.
