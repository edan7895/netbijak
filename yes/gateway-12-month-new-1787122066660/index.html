✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent choice for smaller households or studio apartment dwellers who want high-speed 5G connectivity without the hassle of fiber installation cables. While the heavy discount on the router offers significant entry-level savings, be mindful that you are locked into a 12-month commitment, so verify your area's 5G signal consistency before signing up. The 500GB 4G data backup is a thoughtful inclusion, but since it is not unlimited, power users should monitor their consumption if they frequently venture out of 5G coverage zones. Given the 7-day return policy, we recommend testing the connection across different rooms during the first week to ensure the router placement provides optimal signal strength.
