✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is a strong contender for small households or light-to-moderate users who prioritize low monthly overheads over the permanence of fiber broadband. While the free high-end 5G router adds significant immediate value, remember that you are locked into a 24-month commitment, so it is crucial to verify your home’s 5G signal strength before signing up. The 500GB 4G data allowance serves as a reliable safety net, though heavy users who frequently exceed this cap may eventually find fiber connections more consistent for long-term reliability. We recommend taking advantage of the 7-day return policy to test your device placement thoroughly, as 5G performance can fluctuate significantly based on indoor signal reception.
