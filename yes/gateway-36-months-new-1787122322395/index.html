✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent fit for small households or light-usage renters who need reliable, high-speed connectivity without the hassle of fiber installation. While the complimentary premium router adds significant upfront value, the 36-month commitment is quite lengthy, so ensure your current living situation is stable before locking in. If you live in an area with solid Yes 5G coverage, this offers a seamless plug-and-play experience that rivals entry-level fiber, provided you are comfortable with the strict long-term contract. It is worth testing the signal strength thoroughly during the first seven days to confirm that your specific indoor location can consistently pull those advertised 5G speeds.
