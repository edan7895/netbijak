✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is a strong contender for mid-sized households that need reliable connectivity for streaming and remote work without overpaying for excessive bandwidth. While the inclusion of a Wi-Fi 6 router and the six-month waiver significantly sweetens the deal, keep in mind that you are locked into a two-year commitment, so ensure this tier aligns with your long-term internet habits before signing. If you have many users simultaneously uploading large files or hosting video calls, note that the 50Mbps upload speed is functional but may feel slightly restrictive compared to higher-tier symmetric plans. Most users will find this the ideal sweet spot for daily home use, but evaluate if your specific needs require a faster upload capacity before committing to the full contract term.
