✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent entry-level choice for small households or light internet users who prioritize a stable connection for daily browsing and standard-definition streaming without overpaying for bandwidth they do not need. While the three-month waiver and included WiFi 6 router offer great upfront value, be mindful that you are committing to a two-year contract, so ensure your current address is stable before signing up. For most users, this speed is perfectly adequate for simultaneous usage on a few devices, but if you frequently transfer large files or engage in high-demand gaming, you may want to consider whether a higher tier provides better long-term scalability for your needs.
