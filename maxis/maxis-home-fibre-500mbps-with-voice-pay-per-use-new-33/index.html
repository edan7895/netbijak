✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan hits a sweet spot for medium-sized households where multiple people are simultaneously streaming 4K content, attending video calls, and gaming. The inclusion of a Wi-Fi 6 router is a significant benefit, as it ensures you have the necessary hardware to actually distribute those speeds throughout your home without a bottleneck. While the three-month waiver provides excellent upfront savings, remember that you are committing to a two-year lock-in period, so ensure your current residence is stable before signing up. If you anticipate your internet needs growing significantly in the next few years, verify if the provider offers a seamless upgrade path to gigabit tiers later on, as switching mid-contract can often be complicated.
