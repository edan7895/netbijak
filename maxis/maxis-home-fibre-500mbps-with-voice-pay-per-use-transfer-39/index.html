✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent fit for medium-sized households where multiple people frequently stream 4K content or engage in competitive online gaming simultaneously. While the six-month waiver significantly lowers the effective annual cost, be mindful that the 24-month lock-in period makes this a long-term commitment best suited for those who do not anticipate moving soon. Because the upload speed is capped at 100Mbps, power users who frequently upload large media files for work might find the asymmetric connection limiting compared to symmetrical gigabit options. Before signing up, verify the fiber coverage in your specific residential block to ensure you can take full advantage of the included Wi-Fi 6 hardware for stable indoor connectivity.
