✨ NetBijak's Take

This 1Gbps plan is a powerhouse solution for large households or power users who regularly handle high-bandwidth tasks like 4K streaming, cloud gaming, and heavy file transfers simultaneously. While the provided Wi-Fi 6 router is a solid inclusion, users living in sprawling or multi-story homes should consider budgeting for a mesh system to fully utilize the bandwidth across every corner of the house. Given the 24-month lock-in period, ensure this is a long-term commitment for your residence, as the monthly investment is best leveraged by families who can truly saturate the connection with multiple heavy-duty devices.
