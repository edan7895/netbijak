✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is a strong entry-level choice for small households or young couples who primarily use the internet for high-definition streaming and browsing without heavy multi-device demands. While the Wi-Fi 6 router provides a modern performance boost, keep in mind that the 24-month contract locks you in for a significant period, making this best for those who plan to stay at their current residence for the long haul. Before signing up, ensure that your specific apartment or housing area has strong CelcomDigi fibre coverage, as actual stability can vary significantly based on local infrastructure. If you anticipate your household growing or your bandwidth needs increasing soon, it may be worth checking if a mid-tier plan offers a better price-per-Mbps ratio to avoid an early upgrade fee.
