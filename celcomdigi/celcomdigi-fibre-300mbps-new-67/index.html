✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan strikes an ideal balance for medium-sized households or families who frequently stream 4K content and engage in simultaneous video calls. While the bundled Wi-Fi 6 router ensures modern devices perform at their peak, keep in mind that the 24-month commitment is a long-term lock-in, so ensure your area has stable coverage before signing. It offers great value for the price point, but if you are a heavy content creator or frequently upload large files, you may find the 50Mbps upload speed limiting compared to higher-tier symmetric plans.
