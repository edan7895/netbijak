✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is essentially overkill for standard home offices and is better suited for data-heavy small businesses, such as creative studios or tech startups that handle large file transfers and multiple simultaneous cloud uploads. While the inclusion of Wi-Fi 7 hardware future-proofs your connectivity, remember that you will need compatible devices to actually experience those speeds, making this an investment that requires upgrading your internal network infrastructure. Because you are locked into a 24-month contract, ensure your business location is stable, as the upfront cost of early termination can easily negate the value gained from the bundled voice services and free installation.
