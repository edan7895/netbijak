✨ NetBijak's Take

The Unifi Business Exclusive 500M is an excellent middle-ground solution for small offices or home-based businesses that rely heavily on cloud applications and video conferencing. While the 100Mbps upload speed is adequate for typical business tasks, keep in mind that the 24-month commitment is a long-term investment, so ensure your business location is stable before signing up. The inclusion of Wi-Fi 6 mesh hardware significantly boosts coverage for larger office layouts, making this a high-value package if you need reliable connectivity across multiple rooms. Before finalizing, verify the total cost of ownership against lower-tier plans, as you might find a 300Mbps plan sufficient if your daily operations do not involve heavy large-file transfers.
