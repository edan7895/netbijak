This plan is an ideal entry-level choice for small home offices or solo entrepreneurs who prioritize consistent connectivity, as the included 4G backup provides a crucial safety net against fiber outages. While the monthly cost is competitive, remember that you are committing to a two-year contract, so ensure this address is your long-term base before signing.
