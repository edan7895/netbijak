✨ NetBijak's Take

This plan is an excellent choice for home-based entrepreneurs or small office setups that demand reliable connectivity for video conferencing and cloud-based tasks. The inclusion of a 4G wireless backup is a significant advantage for businesses that cannot afford downtime, effectively mitigating the frustration of fibre outages. While the 24-month contract locks you in for a substantial period, the addition of a business-grade WiFi 6 router and an included phone line provides strong overall value for the price. Before signing up, ensure your premises have decent mobile coverage, as the effectiveness of the automatic backup feature relies entirely on a stable 4G signal.
