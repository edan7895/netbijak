Lowest Ping for Mobile Legends: Unifi, Maxis, or TIME Fibre?

✨ NetBijak AI Summary

Recent third-party testing in Kuala Lumpur shows that TIME Fibre offers the lowest average ping for Mobile Legends at 5–15ms, followed by Maxis at 10–25ms and Unifi at 12–30ms. However, these figures are specific to Kuala Lumpur and do not guarantee the same performance across all locations in Malaysia. Factors such as your geographical location, home router placement, and network congestion play a significant role in your actual gaming latency. While TIME currently shows the strongest test results, players should prioritize connection stability and packet loss over raw download speeds. Before switching providers, it is recommended to test your current connection during peak gaming hours and confirm service availability at your specific address. Ultimately, the best ISP for gaming depends more on local infrastructure and network routing than general advertised performance.

Which Fibre Gives the Lowest Mobile Legends Ping?

Based on available 2026 third-party testing in Kuala Lumpur, TIME recorded the lowest average Mobile Legends ping among TIME, Maxis Fibre and Unifi— but this is not a guaranteed nationwide ranking.

A recent Malaysian ISP gaming test reported these approximate KL results for Mobile Legends Bang Bang:

TIME vs Maxis vs Unifi: What Do the Numbers Tell Us?

TIME Fibre

TIME recorded the lowest range in the available KL test, at around 5–15ms.

TIME also reported a 4ms minimum network latency in FY2025 based on Ookla Speedtest Intelligence data collected from 21 October to 21 November 2025. However, this is a general network latency measurement — not Mobile Legends ping. (TIME)

Maxis Fibre

The same third-party MLBB test recorded around 10–25ms for Maxis Fibre.

Maxis itself explains that latency is particularly important for gaming and recommends wired LAN connections for more consistent performance because Wi-Fi can be affected by walls, interference and other devices. (Maxis)

Unifi

The third-party test recorded around 12–30ms for Unifi.

Unifi also publishes gaming guidance that highlights latency, jitter and connection stability as important factors for online gaming. Its own fibre speed-test examples show low single-digit ping to TM’s test servers, but that should not be treated as Mobile Legends server ping. (Unifi)

Why Your Ping May Be Different

The ISP with the lowest average result does not necessarily give you the lowest ping at home.

Your actual MLBB ping can be affected by:

  1. Your location in Malaysia
  2. ISP routing to the game server
  3. The server used for your particular match
  4. Wi-Fi interference
  5. Router placement
  6. Other devices using your connection
  7. Network congestion and temporary routing changes

This is why a TIME user in KL may get a different result from a TIME user in Johor, Penang or Melaka.

What Should Malaysian MLBB Players Choose?

If lowest ping is your priority, the available KL testing currently gives TIME the strongest result among the three.

But the difference between 5ms and 15ms is not necessarily more important than connection stability, packet loss or whether the ISP is actually available at your building.

Before switching:

  1. Test your current ping during your normal gaming hours.
  2. Check packet loss and ping spikes, not just the lowest number.
  3. If possible, compare using a LAN connection.
  4. Check fibre availability at your exact address.
  5. Do not choose a plan simply because it offers a higher Mbps speed.
