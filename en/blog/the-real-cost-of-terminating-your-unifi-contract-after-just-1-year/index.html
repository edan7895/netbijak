The Real Cost of Terminating Your Unifi Contract After Just 1 Year

✨ NetBijak AI Summary

Terminating a Unifi broadband contract before the 24-month period expires results in a penalty equal to the full subscription fee for all remaining months. Keep in mind that cancellation is not immediate, as Unifi requires a mandatory 30-day notice period before the service is officially terminated. If your contract includes a bundled smart device, you will likely face additional termination charges based on the device’s retail value and remaining months. While some older contracts required the return of hardware like the BTU, current terms generally do not, though it is best to verify your specific agreement. Before initiating the process, confirm your exact outstanding balance and contract status with Unifi to avoid unexpected costs. Carefully reviewing these terms prevents unnecessary charges and ensures a smooth transition when cancelling your subscription.

Cancel Unifi After 1 Year? Here’s the Cost

In Malaysia, Unifi Home plans commonly come with a 24-month contract. If you terminate before the contract ends, Unifi states that the early termination penalty is the full subscription fee for the remaining months of the contract period. (Unifi)

A Simple Example

Calculation
Contract24 months
Used12 months
Remaining12 months
Monthly feeRM139
Basic early termination penaltyRM139 × 12 = RM1,668

So, if your applicable monthly subscription fee is RM139 and you terminate with 12 months remaining, the basic broadband early termination penalty would be RM1,668.

Your actual amount depends on your contract and applicable subscription fee.

Unifi’s Official Calculation

Unifi’s published ETP example states that the penalty is based on the full subscription fee for the remaining months, calculated from the termination process date. (Unifi)

The basic calculation is:

Remaining contract months × applicable monthly subscription fee

For example:

5 months remaining × RM119 = RM595

The 30-Day Notice Most People Miss

Unifi requires customers to give 30 days’ noticebefore terminating their service. After you submit the request, TM says it will provide a pre-final bill and a representative will contact you within seven days to confirm the request. Once payment is settled, the termination order is processed on Day 31 from the notice date. (Unifi)

This matters because cancellation is not immediate.

For example, if you submit your termination request while your contract still has 12 months remaining, the service continues during the notice period. The final early termination calculation is based on the remaining contract period from the termination process date. Unifi’s ETP document specifically states that the calculation uses the termination process date. (Unifi)

So don’t assume:

“I submitted my cancellation today, so my contract ends today.”

It doesn’t.

Got a Smart Device With Your Unifi Plan?

This is where the final bill can be higher.

If your contract includes a smart device, such as a TV, the device may have its own early termination charge depending on the terms of your specific offer.

Unifi’s published smart-device terms have separate calculations for device-related early termination charges. (Unifi)

For example, an Unifi-published campaign example for a Samsung TV with an RRP of RM3,499 and five months remaining calculated the device charge as:

(RM3,499 ÷ 36) × 5 = RM485.97

Combined with a RM595 broadband termination charge, that example produced a total of RM1,080.97.

The exact calculation depends on the terms attached to your specific Unifi offer.

What About the BTU / Modem?

This is one area where you should check your specific contract instead of relying on old advice online.

Unifi’s current FAQ states that after termination, customers do not have to return their devices. (Unifi)

However, older Unifi Home FAQ documents had different equipment-return instructions, including a seven-day BTU return requirement after termination. (Unifi)

So if you see advice claiming:

“You must return your BTU within seven days or automatically pay RM500.”

don’t assume that rule applies to every current Unifi Home customer.

Check the terms of your actual plan or campaign before disposing of any equipment.

Before Cancelling Unifi

Check these four things before submitting your termination request:

  1. Contract end date
  2. Remaining contract months
  3. Your actual monthly subscription fee
  4. Any smart device or contracted add-on

And remember:

30-day notice applies.

Unifi says your termination order is processed on Day 31, not immediately when you submit the request. Make sure your outstanding balance is settled, because Unifi states that an unpaid balance by Day 30 can cause the termination request to be cancelled and the service and charges to continue. (Unifi)

If you are unsure about the final amount, ask Unifi to confirm the exact early termination amount before submitting your cancellation request.

Also, don’t rely on old BTU-return advice without checking your current plan’s terms.
