Unifi vs Maxis Fibre: Who Has the Better Customer Service Response Time?

✨ NetBijak AI Summary

When choosing between Unifi and Maxis Fibre, it is essential to distinguish between rapid customer service replies and actual physical network restoration. Unifi offers a more transparent restoration commitment, explicitly promising to fix eligible connection outages within 24 hours or provide a RM50 bill rebate. In contrast, Maxis prioritizes high-accessibility digital support, offering 24/7 WhatsApp assistance and AI tools, though their formal response commitment for service issues is 48 hours. A critical factor for Maxis users is their reliance on third-party infrastructure, including TM’s network, which can add layers of coordination when physical repairs are required. While Unifi benefits from its direct integration with the underlying TM network for faster issue tracking, Maxis remains a strong choice for those who value diverse and responsive communication channels. Ultimately, Unifi’s published restoration guarantee provides more concrete security for users, while Maxis excels in the speed and availability of its customer support interfaces.

When your Fibre goes down, a fast customer-service reply is not the same thing as getting your internet fixed quickly. For Malaysian users, the bigger question is what happens after the complaint becomes a technical ticket.

NetBijak’s Exclusive Check: Don’t Compare “Reply Time” Alone

Before deciding which ISP has better customer service, look at three stages:

Customer reply → Technical ticket → Actual restoration

An ISP can answer your WhatsApp quickly and still take much longer to restore a physical Fibre fault.

That is why NetBijak compared the actual support commitments and network arrangements published by Unifi and Maxis.

Unifi: A Clear 24-Hour Restoration Commitment

Unifi currently publishes a 24-hour Service Restoration Guarantee. Once an official downtime complaint or trouble ticket has been created, Unifi says it will restore the connection within 24 hours. If it fails to do so, eligible customers can receive a RM50 bill rebate. Unifi Promise

But there is a catch that many customers miss:

The RM50 is not simply credited to everyone automatically.

Eligible customers receive an SMS and MyUnifi notification with the redemption instructions, and the rebate has to be redeemed through the MyUnifi app within the stated redemption period. The benefit applies to eligible Unifi broadband packages of 100Mbps and above. Unifi 24-Hour Restoration FAQ

Not every outage qualifies either. Unifi's published terms contain exclusions, including certain Force Majeure situations such as lightning and other circumstances outside the covered service-restoration conditions.

Still, Unifi's support process is relatively clear. EasyFix can run self-diagnosis, automatically create a ticket when further assistance is needed, or provide access to Live Chat. Customers can also track technical requests and technician details through MyUnifi. Unifi Promise

The important point: Unifi is not merely promising to “reply within 24 hours”.

It is publishing a service restoration target.

Maxis: Fast Digital Access, But Physical Repair Is a Different Story

Maxis provides 24/7 WhatsApp support, and its Maxis AI is also available 24/7 through the Maxis App. Maxis Contact Us · Maxis AI

That is convenient, especially when your internet dies outside normal hours.

But don't confuse 24/7 digital support with 24/7 human technical repair.

Maxis' current Customer Complaint Management Process says billing, network and service-connectivity enquiries will receive a response within 48 hours. Customers can also use WhatsApp to check an existing case or report network and service-connectivity issues. Maxis Contact Us

For some Fibre faults, Maxis also has a more specific technical process. Its Fibre Proactive Alert FAQ says that when a detected hardware or cable-break issue is submitted between 9am and 6pm, the technical team will call within 2 hours to assist and arrange a technical visit. Maxis Fibre Proactive Alert

So Maxis can respond quickly. The bigger issue is who controls the physical network that actually needs fixing.

The TM Network Dependency Most Users Don't See

Maxis Fibre does not run entirely on Maxis-owned fibre infrastructure.

Maxis' own provider information lists TM-HSBA as one of the networks used for Maxis Fibre, alongside Maxis-NGBB, Allo, SACOFA, CTS and TIME.

Maxis has also publicly confirmed that it extended its agreement with TM to continue accessing TM's national fibre network until 2029, alongside its own fibre network. Maxis–TM Fibre Partnership

This creates an important practical difference.

If a physical fault happens on infrastructure provided through TM-HSBA, Maxis may need to coordinate with the underlying network provider before the physical repair can be completed.

So your Maxis WhatsApp reply can come quickly while the actual repair is still waiting for the network-side team to carry out the work.

In simple Malaysian terms:

Maxis customer service can open the case. They may not control every metre of fibre involved in fixing the fault.

That extra coordination layer can matter when the problem is a damaged cable, outside plant fault or other physical infrastructure issue.

Unifi Has One Structural Advantage

Unifi's 24-hour restoration commitment is tied directly to an official downtime report, and Unifi provides service tracking through MyUnifi. It can also proactively notify customers when a network fault is detected on the TM side and allow the customer to confirm the restoration ticket digitally. Unifi Promise

There is less uncertainty about the basic escalation path because Unifi and TM's fixed-line network are closely integrated.

That does not mean every Unifi fault will be repaired within 24 hours. The guarantee has eligibility rules and exclusions. But there is a clearly published target for eligible cases.

Maxis Has a Different Strength

Maxis' strength is accessibility.

You can start with WhatsApp, use Maxis AI, call customer service, or use the Maxis App. The current contact page also gives customers a formal 48-hour response commitment for billing, network and service-connectivity enquiries. Maxis Contact Us

For selected proactive fault cases submitted during its stated daytime window, Maxis also publishes a 2-hour technical-team call-back target. Maxis Fibre Proactive Alert

The limitation is that none of these published response channels is the same as a guaranteed Fibre restoration time.

So, Who Has Better Customer Service?

For service restoration, Unifi has the stronger published commitment because it explicitly promises restoration within 24 hours for eligible cases and provides a rebate when that commitment is missed. Unifi Promise

For support accessibility, Maxis is strong because customers have 24/7 digital support, WhatsApp, app-based assistance and a published 48-hour response process for complaints and service-connectivity matters. Maxis Contact Us

But when the fault is physical, the network behind your Fibre matters.

A quick Maxis reply does not necessarily mean a quick physical repair when the affected infrastructure requires coordination with an underlying network provider.

NetBijak’s Take

For customers who care most about getting a Fibre fault restored, Unifi currently has the clearer public commitment.

Maxis is attractive for its 24/7 digital support, but don't judge its technical service purely by how quickly WhatsApp answers you. With Fibre, the real test is what happens after the ticket is created.

And for Unifi customers, remember the RM50 headline:

It is a conditional rebate, not automatic cash compensation. You need to qualify and redeem it through MyUnifi.
