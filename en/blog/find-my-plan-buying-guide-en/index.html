How to Choose the Right Broadband Plan for Your Home in Malaysia

1. Start With Your Household

Think about how many people live in your home — 1-2 people, 3-4 people, or 5+ people. But household size is only one factor among several that determine the right plan for you.

2. Consider What You Actually Do Online

Your daily habits matter as much as headcount: streaming, gaming, working from home, online learning, video calls, smart home devices, and CCTV all use your connection differently. Think about your household's real usage pattern, not just the fastest speed available.

3. How Much Internet Speed Do You Need?

Speed/Suitable For
100MbpsLight users / small households
300MbpsEveryday family use
500MbpsMultiple users / heavier usage
1Gbps+Heavy users / many devices

The right speed is the one that matches your actual usage — not necessarily the fastest number available.

4. Home Type Matters

Landed homes and high-rise residences can have different considerations — home size, number of rooms, WiFi coverage, router placement, and whether mesh WiFi is needed all affect your actual experience, on top of which providers are available for your building.

5. Think About Your Budget

Don't choose a plan simply because it has the highest speed. Look at the full picture: monthly price, speed, included equipment, contract length, and current promotions — not just the Mbps number.

6. Check the Contract

Before signing up, check the contract period, installation fees, included equipment, early termination penalties, and any conditions attached to promotional pricing. These all affect your actual cost over time.

7. Don't Confuse Internet Speed With WiFi Coverage

Subscribing to a 1Gbps plan doesn't mean every device in your home will actually reach 1Gbps. Your WiFi router, distance from it, walls, and number of connected devices all affect your real-world experience — router placement and home layout matter just as much as your plan's advertised speed.
