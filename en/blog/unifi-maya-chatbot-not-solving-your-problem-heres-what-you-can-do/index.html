Unifi Maya Chatbot Not Solving Your Problem? Here’s What You Can Do

✨ NetBijak AI Summary

Stuck in a loop with Unifi’s Maya chatbot? While automated tools are useful for basic tasks, they often fail to resolve complex billing or technical issues that require human intervention. This guide outlines practical alternatives, including reaching out via Facebook Messenger or the official Unifi app for documented support. If those fail, calling 100 or visiting a physical TMpoint allows you to speak directly with staff for more pressing matters. Always remember to document your reference numbers and maintain a log of your communication to ensure your case is tracked properly. Should all else fail, you can escalate unresolved complaints through the MCMC consumer portal for further assistance.


If Unifi’s Maya chatbot keeps showing you options without addressing your actual problem, try another support channel instead of repeating the same steps.

Automated chat can be useful for basic enquiries, but it becomes frustrating when you have a specific billing dispute, connection problem or complaint that needs human attention.

Here are some practical alternatives based on real user experiences and Unifi’s published support channels.

1. Send Unifi a Private Message on Facebook

One alternative is to contact the official Unifi Facebook page directly.

In my own experience, I sent a private message explaining my issue and eventually got it resolved. However, it took time, so this is not necessarily the best option if you need an immediate response.

Official Facebook page: facebook.com/weareunifi

To make your message more useful, include:

  1. A clear explanation of what went wrong.
  2. Your existing complaint or report reference number, if available.
  3. The steps you have already taken.
  4. The specific action or resolution you are requesting.

Avoid posting your account number, identity card details or other sensitive information publicly. Use private messaging for account-related matters.

2. Try Submitting Your Complaint Through the Unifi App

The Unifi app provides support options, including troubleshooting and access to live chat. A friend also told me that they previously submitted a complaint through the UniVerse app and received a response.

That does not guarantee the same response time for every customer, but it is another channel worth trying if the chatbot is not helping.

You can refer to Unifi’s official guide here:

MyUnifi App Support Guide

When submitting a complaint, explain the problem clearly and keep a record of any reference number or response. This makes it easier to follow up without having to explain the entire situation again.

3. Call Unifi Customer Service at 100

If the problem involves an internet outage, billing issue or unresolved technical fault, calling may be more practical than waiting for a social media reply.

Unifi lists 100 as its customer service number for calls from local fixed-line or mobile phones.

According to its official contact page, technical assistance is available daily from 8am to 10pm. Product, service, order and billing enquiries are listed as available daily from 8am to 5:30pm.

Check the latest details here: Unifi Contact Us

Before calling, prepare your account details, a short timeline of the issue and any existing report number. Ask the agent what action will be taken and when you should follow up if the issue remains unresolved.

4. Visit a TMpoint or Unifi Store for Urgent Issues

If online support has not helped and the issue is urgent, consider visiting a nearby TMpoint or Unifi Store.

In-person assistance gives you an opportunity to explain the situation directly to staff and show supporting documents. However, you will need to spend time travelling and may have to queue. A visit also does not guarantee that the issue will be resolved immediately, especially if it requires technical investigation.

Bring your relevant bills, screenshots, previous complaint references and any messages from support. Before leaving, ask for a complaint reference and confirmation of the next steps.

Check the official Unifi contact page for current outlet information.

These are practical suggestions, not a guaranteed ranking of response speed. The best channel depends on the type of issue and whether it needs technical investigation.

What If Unifi Still Does Not Resolve Your Complaint?

Keep your complaint reference number, screenshots, dates of contact and any replies you receive. If the issue remains unresolved after you have contacted Unifi, you can check the Malaysian Communications and Multimedia Commission’s official complaints portal:

MCMC Consumer Redress Portal

Provide a clear timeline and include the records of your previous attempts to resolve the matter.

NetBijak Tips

You do not have to keep going around in circles with a chatbot if it is not addressing your problem.

Try another official channel, explain the issue clearly and keep a record of every complaint. Facebook messaging may work, the app may provide another route, and calling or visiting a TMpoint may be more suitable for certain cases.

The important thing is to make sure your complaint is properly recorded and that you know what the next step is.
